Stories from our community partners

North Carolina's health plans work alongside community organizations across the state. These short videos share two of their stories, in their own words.

Poe Center for Health Education

Care that extends beyond the exam room

Summer is considered the hungriest time of the year for many local children. The Poe Center's Executive Director Ann Rollins and health educator Cassie Cubilla share how partnerships with managed care help the center reach as many children as possible.

North Carolina's managed care organizations provided over $1.25 million in funding to the Poe Center's work.

Raleigh, N.C.

Feeding Avery Families

Avery County is resilient

When Hurricane Helene hit Avery County, the community came together. Feeding Avery Families Executive Director Bonnie Clark shares how volunteers, churches and nonprofits stepped up, and how the food pantry grew from serving about 550 households a month to nearly 1,000.

North Carolina's managed care organizations provided over $55M in support for Hurricane Helene recovery.

Avery County, N.C.
