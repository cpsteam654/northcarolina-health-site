The North Carolina Association of Health Plans (NCAHP) has represented companies providing health insurance to North Carolinians for over 20 years. We advocate for a vibrant health care marketplace that provides affordable, high quality care to consumers.

NCAHP is the leading voice for innovative, market-oriented health care solutions in North Carolina. Recently, NCAHP was instrumental in passing North Carolina’s Medicaid reform legislation and are working with state leaders to ensure the successful implementation of this historic reform that accomplishes its goals of budget predictability for taxpayers and choice for Medicaid beneficiaries. NCAHP is also engaged in policy initiatives important to commercial health insurers.

Our members are committed to the health and financial well-being of health care consumers. As their voice in Raleigh, NCAHP is a forceful advocate for policies that ensure a level playing field for health plans and affordable access to the best possible care for North Carolinians.
