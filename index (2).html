Peter T. Daniel, Sr. to Retire from NC Association of Health Plans; Nathan Babcock Named Executive Director

Babcock, longtime senior advisor to Senate leadership who helped advance North Carolina's historic Medicaid expansion and health care reform legislation, takes helm November 15.

For immediate release, November 23, 2026

RALEIGH, N.C. (November 23, 2026) — Peter T. Daniel, Sr., who has served as executive director of the North Carolina Association of Health Plans (NCAHP) for the past five years, will retire from the association effective Nov. 30, 2026.

The NCAHP Board of Directors has named Nathan Babcock, a longtime senior advisor in the North Carolina Senate, as his successor.

“Serving North Carolina's health plans these past five years has been a great privilege,” Daniel said. “Together we built an association that legislators, regulators and partners trust to bring facts and good faith to the table. Some of our most important work was reminding lawmakers that every regulation added to health coverage carries a cost. That cost has to be captured in the marketplace, and it ultimately shows up in the premiums paid by employers, families and individuals. I have always believed in leading by serving the people around you, and I leave knowing NCAHP is in exceptional hands. Nathan helped shape one of the most consequential health care laws in our state's history, and no one is better prepared to lead our members through what comes next.”

Babcock brings 16 years of experience in North Carolina politics, public policy and government affairs to his new role with NCAHP. As a senior advisor to Senate leadership, he played a key role in advancing major legislative initiatives, including North Carolina's historic 2023 Medicaid expansion and health care reform legislation.

Babcock will assume the executive director role November 15, 2026. Daniel will work closely with him in the coming weeks to ensure a seamless transition for member plans and partners.

Under Daniel's leadership, NCAHP was rebuilt into one of the most respected policy voices in Raleigh. He advised the association's member plans during the early years of Medicaid managed care and the December 2023 launch of Medicaid expansion.

Daniel also led NCAHP through a tumultuous stretch for commercial health insurance. He invested a tremendous amount of time keeping lawmakers mindful of how proposed legislation can drive up costs for the businesses, families and individuals who pay premiums.

Daniel came to NCAHP after a long career in North Carolina government, nonprofit leadership and association management, including nearly 25 years as the second-ranking executive of the North Carolina Farm Bureau Federation. He will remain a familiar face at the General Assembly and in Washington, where he will continue advocating for North Carolina businesses, farmers and rural communities.

“Peter took an organization that needed rebuilding and made it a voice that policymakers seek out,” said Chris Paterson, NCAHP Board President and CEO at Carolina Complete Health. “The board is deeply grateful for his leadership, his steady counsel and the relationships he built on our members' behalf. Nathan knows the General Assembly from the inside and understands the Medicaid program our members serve. He was also a driving force behind the Senate's stand against rising health insurance premiums. We are confident he will build on the strong foundation Peter leaves behind.”

Babcock began his career with the North Carolina Republican Senate Caucus in 2010, becoming its Political Director in 2011. He later served as Political Director of the NC Chamber and founded Atlas Politics, a political and public affairs consulting firm.

“I'm honored by the board's confidence and grateful to Peter for the strong foundation he has built,” Babcock said. “North Carolina's health plans serve millions of people through Medicaid and the commercial market, and both face significant challenges in the years ahead. I look forward to working with our members, lawmakers, and partners to advance policies that lower health care costs, protect access to quality care, and ensure North Carolinians have affordable coverage.”

About NCAHP

The North Carolina Association of Health Plans is the statewide voice for the health plans that cover North Carolinians through Medicaid managed care and the commercial market. NCAHP advocates for policies that keep quality health coverage affordable and accessible.
